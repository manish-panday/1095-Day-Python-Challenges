Today's Challenge: Online Shopping Discount Calculator
Background

You are building a simple checkout system for an online store.

The store gives customers different discounts depending on the amount they spend.

Your program must calculate the discount and final price.

Requirements

Ask the customer for:

Customer's full name
Product price
Quantity

Calculate:

Total Price = Product Price × Quantity

Then determine the discount based on the total price.

Discount Rules
Total Price	Discount
10,000 or more	20%
5,000–9,999	10%
2,000–4,999	5%
Below 2,000	0%

Then calculate:

Discount Amount = Total Price × Discount / 100

and:

Final Price = Total Price - Discount Amount
Expected Output
Example 1

Input:

Enter your full name: Manish Panday
Enter product price: 3000
Enter quantity: 2

Output:

========== SHOPPING RECEIPT ==========

Customer: Manish Panday

Product Price: 3000
Quantity: 2

Total Price: 6000
Discount: 10%
Discount Amount: 600

Final Price: 5400

Bonus Challenge

Add a VIP customer option.

Ask:

Are you a VIP customer? (yes/no):

VIP customers receive an additional 5% discount.

For example:

Total Price: 6000
Normal Discount: 10%
VIP Discount: 5%
Total Discount: 15%
Final Price: 5100
Important:

Don't simply subtract 5 from the final price.

Think carefully about what 5% actually means.

Hints
Hint 1

Calculate the total before determining the discount.

Hint 2

Store the discount percentage in a variable such as:

discount_percent
Hint 3

Calculate the discount amount separately.

Hint 4

Try to print the receipt only once.

Hint 5

Think about the order of your if/elif conditions. Start with the highest price range.

Think Before Coding

Create this logic in your head first:

Product Price

Discount Amount

Common Mistakes

Watch out for:

Calculating the discount from the product price instead of the total price.
Forgetting to multiply price × quantity.
Confusing 10% with 10.
Subtracting the discount percentage directly from the price.
Putting the discount conditions in the wrong order.
Repeating the receipt three or four times.
